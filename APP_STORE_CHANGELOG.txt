New
- Ordered read lists can now continue like series: opt in once, and any book in a list navigates in list order, with a Read Lists in Progress dashboard section showing each list's next book. Reading state syncs across your devices.
- Dashboard sections get their own card styles: switch any section between large, small, and horizontal cards right from its header, with a new medium size in between, plus a setting to hide the style menu.
- Browse pages get a three-way layout menu: Row, Medium, or Large grid.
- Detail pages are redesigned around a compact hero and a single action card, with a two-column layout on iPad and Mac, collapsible metadata sections, and a prominent two-line Read button showing your progress.
- Failed page loads in the reader now explain what went wrong and offer Retry instead of spinning forever.
- Reading progress is only recorded after a few pages are turned (configurable), so accidentally opening a book no longer marks it as started or wipes a finished book's state.
- Fresh default app icon: gradient glyph on white, with Classic and Legacy alternates still available.
- Oneshot cards now show a book icon and the primary author instead of a bare label.

Improvements
- Horizontal dashboard cards are tinted by the cover color, carry download status and a menu button, and match Apple Books sizing; card text and badges scale with card size.
- The custom theme color is retired in favor of a clean monochrome look that follows the system.
- Offline-first reading and auto-delete toggles moved into Reader settings, and section downloads moved onto each dashboard section's page with Download Latest 20 and Download All.
- The API keys page now only protects the key this device actually uses, instead of locking keys created on your other devices.
- Metadata edit sheets refresh the page right after saving, and their add buttons work reliably.
- Filter and saved filters are now direct toolbar buttons on Browse and Offline pages.
- Smoother pull-to-refresh, and iPad/macOS sidebars reload the whole page when switching collections or read lists.
- KMReader now requires Komga 1.20.0 or later.

Fixes
- Fixed the Show More toggle disappearing on long summaries.
- Fixed dashboard rows going stale on iPad after switching back to Home.
- Fixed the reader's page dimension warning, which could not be dismissed.
- Fixed book lists losing their sort order on some servers.
- Fixed offline next/previous navigation disagreeing with the server for books sharing the same series number.
- Fixed dark-mode cover shadows glowing white and oversized badges on small cards.
- Fixed long-press menus not working on card covers.
- Fixed returning to a detail page resetting its list back to the top.

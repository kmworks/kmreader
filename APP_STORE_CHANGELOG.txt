New
- Panel Mode on iPhone and iPad walks through paged LTR and RTL comics panel by panel. Download the detection model explicitly, then use the dedicated reader toggle to enter or leave the mode.
- Optional volume-button page turns on iOS work across DIVINA, EPUB, and PDF, respecting reading direction and continuous-book navigation.
- Manage AniList, MyAnimeList, Bangumi, and MangaBaka tracker accounts and series links on supported servers. Search providers, review linked series, and update remote reading status and progress.
- Characters, teams, locations, and other notable entities now appear in book and series metadata, with editing and exact kind-and-name browse filters.

Reader
- Panel Mode and manga translation have clear labeled controls, and their configuration is unified under Manga Tools.
- Animated WebP and GIF pages now autoplay in the macOS reader.
- EPUB Core Text rendering improves ruby and emphasized text, vertical-writing images, and other layout details while preserving reading positions.

Browse & Server
- Keep Reading, On Deck, widgets, and next-book suggestions follow the server's recent-reading window and next-unread preference on supported servers, both online and offline.
- Library, Search, and Offline tabs remember their selected library scopes across app restarts.
- Server Info can report available updates on supported servers, and account settings provide session lists for users and administrators.
- Tracker search and linking are clearer, with alternate titles, localized MangaBaka results, and compact linked-series browsing.

Offline & Logs
- Large download queues finish more efficiently, and database failures no longer masquerade as missing downloads or empty queues.
- Launch cleanup handles large offline libraries without exhausting file descriptors or clearing valid downloads when storage cannot be read.
- Log sharing exports every entry matching the current filters as a text file, not just rows already loaded on screen.
- Live server updates resume from the last received event after a temporary disconnect on supported servers.
